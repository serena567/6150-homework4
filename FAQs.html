Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I borrow a book from Vallejo Public Library?

To borrow a book, you must have a valid library card. Simply search for the book in our catalog, and follow the instructions to check it out at the circulation desk or via our online system.

What is the late fee for overdue books?

The late fee for overdue books is $0.25 per day. We recommend returning books on time to avoid any charges.

Can I renew my borrowed books?

Yes, you can renew your borrowed books online through your library account, or in person at the circulation desk, as long as no one else has placed a hold on the item.
